Privacy Policy — Reversible Gate

Effective date: September 1, 2026
Publisher: SATA studio (individual developer)
Contact: contact form

This policy covers Reversible Gate, an app for Atlassian Confluence.

Summary

Reversible Gate does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data or customer content outside of your Atlassian Confluence site.

What the app does

Reversible Gate is a Confluence macro. When you insert it on a page and answer its checklist questions, your answers are saved only in the macro's configuration on that Confluence page, which is stored by Atlassian as part of your page content and governed by Atlassian's own privacy and security terms.

What the app does not do

  • It does not store any data on servers operated by the publisher or by third parties.
  • It does not make network requests to services outside Atlassian.
  • It does not request any Atlassian API scopes and cannot read other pages, users, or spaces.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, tracking, or any AI/LLM service.
  • It does not log end-user data. The app has no backend function.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app stores nothing outside your Confluence page, deleting the macro or the page removes the answers, subject to Confluence's own page history and trash behaviour. Uninstalling the app leaves no data with the publisher.

Children

The app is a workplace tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If the app ever changes to collect or transmit data, this policy and the Marketplace listing will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact form. We respond within 5 business days.
