Reversible Gate — User Guide

App: Reversible Gate (free Confluence macro)
Publisher: SATA studio (individual developer)
Support: contact form — response within 5 business days

Install

  1. Open your Confluence site as an admin → Apps → Find new apps → search "Reversible Gate" → Get app.
  2. No configuration is required. The app requests no permissions.

Use

  1. Edit a page and type /Reversible Gate to insert the macro.
  2. The configuration panel opens. Enter a short task name, then answer the seven questions with Yes / No / Not sure. Add a note if you like.
  3. Save the macro and publish the page. The macro shows the verdict, the reason, the safeguard to apply, and your answers.

The seven questions

  • Undo — can the result be reversed in minutes, by yourself?
  • Outward — does the task publish, send, or share anything outside your team?
  • Destruction — does it delete or overwrite data that has no backup?
  • Money — does it spend money or change a contract or billing?
  • Access — does it change accounts, permissions, or secrets?
  • Blast radius — is the impact contained to a sandbox, draft, or branch?
  • Review — will a human review the result before it takes effect?

Verdicts

VerdictMeaning
GOAll answers are on the safe side. The task can be delegated.
GUARDA soft risk (blast radius or review) is present. Put the listed safeguard in place first, then proceed.
STOPA hard risk (cannot undo, outward, destructive, money, access) or an unanswered "Not sure" is present. A human should perform the irreversible step.
INCOMPLETEAt least one question is unanswered. The macro never shows GO on incomplete answers.

Try it without saving

If you insert the macro and close the panel without saving, the macro shows a "Try it" mode where you can answer on the page. Those answers are not saved.

Privacy

The app stores nothing outside your Confluence page and makes no external requests. See the privacy policy.
